Welcome to Unbound CRM

Getting Started

Unbound CRM brings everything your team needs about a customer into one place, sales activity, orders and invoices, contacts, pricing, quotes and more, so you rarely have to go hunting for information.

It's designed for more than just salespeople. Internal sales, customer service, quality and accounts staff all use it, and because everyone can arrange their own screen, each person can put the information they care about front and centre.

Working alongside SYSPRO

Unbound CRM works hand-in-hand with SYSPRO. A lot of what you see (customer details, orders, invoices, pricing and sales figures) is drawn live from SYSPRO, so it's always up to date. When you edit a key field in the CRM, your change is saved straight back to SYSPRO. Throughout this guide, a SYSPRO tag means the information on that page comes from SYSPRO.

The big ideaSee everything you need in one glance, without clicking through endless tabs. That's the whole point of Unbound CRM.

The same everywhere you work

The CRM looks and works the same on your computer, your phone and even inside Outlook, so there's no second app to learn. See Getting Around for how to use it on mobile, and the Outlook Add-in page for email.

Getting Around: Panels

Getting Started

Information in the CRM is organised into colourful panels. The colours stay the same everywhere, so you quickly learn where to look, for example, Contacts are always green and Attachments are always purple.

Make the screen your own

  • Drag to rearrange. Move panels around so the ones you use most sit at the top.
  • Half-height & stack. Shrink panels to half height and stack them, so you can fit more on screen at once.
  • Stow away. Tuck a panel to the side as a small tab if you don't need it; click the tab to bring it back and drag it into place.

Panel controls

  • Menu (☰). Most panels have a small menu with extra options for that panel.
  • Flip view. A switch button flips a panel over to a related view, for example, Open Orders flips to Invoices, and Contacts flips to Superseded Contacts.

On your phone

On mobile you get the same panels, just tap or swipe through them one at a time instead of seeing them side by side. Nothing is missing and nothing to relearn.

TipThere's a lot on screen by design. Spend a few minutes arranging, stacking and stowing panels the way that suits your role, you only need to do it once.

Info Panel SYSPRO

Working with a Customer

The Info panel is your customer's key details at a glance, name, email, address and more. Most of it comes straight from SYSPRO.

  • Edit in place. Many fields can be edited right here. When you save a change, it's written straight back to SYSPRO and the panel refreshes with the latest details.
  • Show the fields you care about. You decide which fields appear and in what order, see Settings & Customisation.
  • Add your own fields. Track things SYSPRO doesn't, like employee count or a customer's product type, with custom fields.

Tasks Everyday

Working with a Customer

Tasks are how you keep track of what needs doing for a customer, calls to make, quotes to follow up, visits to book.

Creating a task

  • Click New Task and choose a type (and sub-type), for example a call, a meeting or a specific kind of visit.
  • Assign it to yourself or a colleague, give it a name and a comment, attach files, and set a due date.
  • Tag colleagues so they're notified when the task is created and when it's completed, handy for letting your manager know about an important job.

Working your list

  • See all upcoming tasks for the customer. Tasks with no name against them are assigned to you.
  • Add comments as you go, "tried calling, no answer" → "tried again", and push the due date out to follow up later. Call, comment, reschedule, repeat.
  • When you complete a task it becomes a permanent record in the Interactions feed, keeping all your comments and files with it.

Interactions (Feed)

Working with a Customer

The Interactions feed is the complete history of everything that's happened with a customer, newest first.

  • Your activity. Completed tasks, notes and calls all land here.
  • Automatic history. Changes made in the CRM are logged automatically, so you can see what changed, when, and who changed it.
  • Quotes. When you email a quote, a copy of the PDF is saved to the feed exactly as it was sent, a handy record of what the customer received and when.
  • Emails. Emails you track from Outlook appear here in full, with their attachments. If the customer replies, use Fetch Conversation to pull the whole thread in. See the Outlook Add-in.

Outlook Add-in

Working with a Customer

The Outlook add-in lets you save important emails against a customer, and even use the whole CRM without leaving your inbox.

Tracking an email

How to track an email
  1. Open the email in Outlook and open the Unbound add-in.
  2. If it suggests the right customer, click the suggested name. Otherwise, type the customer's name.
  3. Click Track, the email (and its attachments) is saved to that customer's Interactions feed.

Works in both desktop and web Outlook.

The whole CRM, inside Outlook

The add-in isn't just for tracking emails, the full CRM runs inside it. You can check your tasks and notifications and open any customer's record right from Outlook.

Details Panel

Working with a Customer

The Details panel holds the fuller picture of a customer, beyond their basic info.

  • Business summary & strategy. Free-text notes on who the customer is and your plan for them.
  • Face-to-face tracking. See how many in-person visits you've had, and how many in the last three months. Set a target frequency, if you fall behind, a friendly sad-face reminds you it's time to reach out.
  • Interaction tracking. The same idea for all interactions (calls, notes, etc.), with a quick breakdown of what those interactions were.
  • Key dates. Last price review date and the anniversary of their first order (as far back as SYSPRO records go).

Working with a Customer

Open Orders

  • See what's on order: items, order quantity, quantity still to come, order date and order number.

Contacts SYSPRO

Working with a Customer

All the people you deal with at a customer, kept in sync with SYSPRO's contacts.

  • Multiple contacts per customer, each with details like position, preferred name, email, phone/fax and birthday.
  • Add a photo and extra notes to a contact if you like, entirely optional.
  • Archive, don't delete. Contacts can't be deleted. Instead you archive (supersede) them, recording why and when, so nothing is ever lost. Use the flip view to see superseded contacts.

Pipeline & Quoting

Working with a Customer

Track the deals you're working on and build quotes for your customers, all in one place.

Two ways to add a deal

  • Opportunity, a quick, simple entry: title, probability, close date, amount and notes. Good for logging an expected value (e.g. "$100,000 expected in September, at tender stage").
  • Quote, a full quote built line by line, with live pricing (below).

Stages & weighted value

  • Deals move through stages (for example Open, Demo Completed, Contract Supplied), and each stage carries a probability. You can override the probability on any deal.
  • Probability gives you a weighted value: a $100,000 deal at 50% counts as $50,000 in your forecast, a realistic view of what's likely to land.
  • If you sell across divisions or product types, you can have separate categories, each with their own stages and quote layout.

Building a quote

Steps
  1. Enter a title, close date and any notes.
  2. Add a quote number, an expiry date and the delivery address (pulled from SYSPRO, editable if needed), plus an attention line.
  3. Add line items by stock code and quantity. Prices come live from SYSPRO (including contract pricing, buying groups and quantity price breaks) so your quote matches what SYSPRO would produce.
  4. Apply a further discount if you need to, and add a freight charge.
  5. Click Create.

Once the quote exists

  • Print it, formatted to match your stationery (with a different layout per category if you have them).
  • Email it, a PDF copy is saved to the customer's Interactions feed, timestamped as sent.
  • Create Order Coming soon, turn a won quote into a SYSPRO sales order, which then appears in Open Orders.
NoteQuotes you build here are self-contained in the CRM. They don't currently create a matching quote inside SYSPRO.

Attachments Everyday

Working with a Customer

Keep all a customer's documents and photos together, with version history and quick photo mark-up.

Files & versions

  • Rename any file to something clear, no matter what it was called on your computer.
  • Upload a new version of a file and the old one is kept as a superseded version, so you always have the history.
  • Download history shows who downloaded what and when.
  • Paste to upload: copy files on your computer and press Ctrl + V in the panel to drop them straight in.

Mark up a photo

  • When you add an image, you can crop it and add notes, for example circle a damaged item and write "this one is scratched".
  • The marked-up version is what gets saved, so the note travels with the photo. Ideal on your phone when you're on site.
  • The same crop-and-note tool works on images you add to tasks and interactions too, handy for sending a photo through to the quality team.

Accounts (Prospects)

Accounts & Projects

An account is a prospect you're working to win. It looks and works just like a customer record, so there's nothing new to learn, it simply leaves out the panels that only apply once someone is trading:

  • No Open Orders
  • No Sales
  • No Pricing
  • No Account Feed

Everything else (Info, Tasks, Interactions, Details, Contacts, Pipeline, Attachments and the AI assistant) is exactly the same. When the prospect starts trading, you convert them into a customer and their whole history comes along.

Projects

Accounts & Projects

Projects are a flexible record for anything that doesn't sit neatly against a single customer. A project has its own Info, Tasks, Interactions and Attachments, and can be linked to a customer, a builder or a prospect.

When to use a project

Use a project whenever information spans more than one party, or when one contact is involved in many separate jobs. For example, if you talk to an architect about several different buildings, each building can be its own project, so the conversations stay neatly separated instead of piling up against the one contact.

Other handy uses

  • Competitors, keep notes and intel on competing products.
  • Marketing activity, a customer's shopfront refit or a conference, linked back to the customer.
  • Anything else, projects are deliberately flexible, and their fields can be tailored to whatever you need to track.

Tasks & Peek Panels

Your Dashboard

Your task list on the dashboard works just like the Tasks panel on a customer, but with a great time-saver called peek panels.

  • Each task has small blue squares. Double-click one to "peek" at that customer's information right there, without opening their full record.
  • Peek their Interactions to remind yourself of your last conversation; peek Contacts and click a number to start a call; peek Info and click the address to open it in maps.
  • Line your tasks up for the day, then use peek panels to get what you need for each one, no navigating away.

Settings & Customisation Admin

Setup & Administration

Settings is where administrators tailor the CRM to your business, which fields show, what data people can pick, and how activity is categorised.

Choose and arrange your fields

  • Rearrange the fields on the Info panel by dragging, put what matters most at the top.
  • Show more SYSPRO fields. Any standard SYSPRO customer field can be added to the panel, each with its own icon (which you can change).
  • Custom SYSPRO fields your business already uses in SYSPRO can be shown here too.
  • Control what's editable. Core fields can be edited in the CRM; others are read-only so they stay controlled in SYSPRO.

Add your own custom fields

How to add a custom field
  1. Create a new field and give it a name (e.g. "Employee count").
  2. Pick an icon and choose who is allowed to edit it.
  3. Choose the field type: text, attachment, or a single-choice list, and more.
  4. Drag it into position and it appears on the Info panel.
Tip: keep your data cleanUse single-choice fields (with set options like "0–50" and "51–100") wherever you can. Because people pick from a list rather than typing freely, your data stays consistent and easy to report on.

Account vs customer fields

You can set up fields separately for accounts (prospects) and customers. They're linked, so when you convert an account to a customer the matching details carry across automatically.

Task & interaction types

Define your own task and interaction types and sub-types. This lets you report not just on how many meetings your team had, but what kind, giving you a much richer view of activity.

Creating & Converting Records

Setup & Administration

Creating a customer

When you create a customer, you fill in the key SYSPRO details (branch, territory, area and so on). This information is sent through to SYSPRO to set the customer up.

Converting an account into a customer

When a prospect starts trading, you turn their account into a customer. What you see depends on your permissions:

  • Administrators see a Convert to New Customer button and can do it straight away.
  • Other users see Request Conversion, which emails your accounts team. They check the details (business number, credit limit and so on) and then complete the conversion.
Nothing is lostConverting brings all the account's history (attachments, tasks and interactions) across to the new customer record.

Following & Notifications

Setup & Administration

Following is the easiest way to stay across the customers and projects that matter to you.

  • Follow a record by clicking the star in its top-left corner.
  • Once you follow a customer or project, you're notified of activity on it, even when you're not directly tagged.
  • For key accounts, following also sends you an email whenever a note is added, and keeps them on your dashboard.

You'll also be notified whenever a colleague tags you in a task or interaction, or completes a task you're tagged in. All of this shows in Notifications on your dashboard.

Tips & Tricks

Reference

  • Arrange your panels once. Drag, stack and stow panels so what you use most is up top, it's saved for you.
  • Use peek panels. On the dashboard, double-click a task's blue squares to glance at a customer's interactions, contacts or address without leaving the page.
  • Paste files with Ctrl + V. Copy files on your computer and paste them straight into Attachments.
  • Mark up photos on site. Crop and annotate a photo on your phone to capture an issue clearly, the note is saved with the image.
  • Follow your key accounts. Star your top customers for email alerts and to keep them on your dashboard.
  • Flip panels. Use the switch button to move between Open Orders and Invoices, or Contacts and Superseded Contacts.
  • Use single-choice fields when adding custom fields, to keep your data tidy and easy to report on.
  • Print documents on the spot. Pro formas, invoices and dispatch notes print in your own stationery, straight from the CRM.
  • Work from Outlook. Track emails and open customer records without leaving your inbox.
